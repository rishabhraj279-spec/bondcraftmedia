Meta Ads

Content gets you noticed. Ads put it in front of the right people.

We run paid campaigns on Instagram and Facebook using the reels we shoot for you, aimed at the people most likely to walk in, call, message or buy. Here is what Meta Ads is, how it works and what it can do for your business.

What is Meta Ads?

Meta is the company behind Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp. Meta Ads is its paid advertising system: you pay to show your videos, photos and offers to people on these apps, and you choose exactly who sees them.

A normal post is seen mostly by people who already follow you. A Meta ad can be shown to people in your city who have never heard of your business, right when they are scrolling Instagram or Facebook. Everything is set up and tracked in Meta's Ads Manager, where results show up in numbers.

Think of it as the difference between putting a poster up inside your own shop and putting one on the busiest road in town, in front of exactly the people most likely to buy.

How Meta Ads work

01

Set a goal

Pick what you want: more views, messages, leads, store visits or sales. Meta then shows your ad to the people most likely to do exactly that.

02

Choose your audience

Select location, age, interests and behaviour, or reach people who already know you, such as past customers and people who watched your videos.

03

Set a budget, add your video

Decide a daily budget and add your reel or photo with a caption and a button. You can start with a small amount and change it any time.

04

Meta delivers, you measure

Each time someone scrolls, Meta runs a quick auction that weighs your budget against how relevant your ad is to that person. Ads Manager then reports the results.

✦

Faster than organic growth

What Meta Ads can do for your business

Reach & Awareness

Put your brand in front of thousands of people in your city, or within a few kilometres of your outlet. Ideal for launches, grand openings, new menus and festive offers.

Followers & Engagement

Promote your best reels and posts to people who are likely to watch, save, share and follow, so your page grows with a real local audience.

Leads & Enquiries

Instant forms inside Instagram and Facebook collect a name and phone number without the customer leaving the app. Works well for real estate, salons, events and services.

WhatsApp & DM Ads

Click-to-message ads open a WhatsApp or Instagram chat in one tap, so bookings, table reservations and product enquiries land straight in your inbox.

Website Sales & Catalog

Drive purchases on your website, or showcase products from your catalog. With the Meta Pixel installed we can track what people view, add to cart and buy.

Precise Targeting

Choose who sees your ad by location, age, gender, interests and behaviour. A café can reach people within 5 km, a jeweller can reach wedding shoppers.

Retargeting & Lookalikes

Reach people who watched your videos, visited your profile or website, or are already on your customer list, then find new people who resemble your best customers.

Testing & Optimisation

Run different videos, hooks, captions and audiences side by side, then move budget to what performs and pause what does not. Every rupee gets smarter over time.

Influencer marketing

People trust people. Let the right creators introduce your business.

We find local creators whose followers look like your customers, brief them, and manage the collaboration from the first message to the final post. You get the trust of a personal recommendation without chasing anyone yourself.

What we handle

  1. Find the right creators

    We shortlist creators whose audience matches your customers in city, interests and style.

  2. Agree the fee

    The influencer quotes their rate. You see it, together with our agency fee, before anything is booked.

  3. Brief and plan

    We share the content idea, your offer, the location and what to show, so the post feels natural and stays on brand.

  4. Shoot and publish

    We coordinate the visit and the posting date, and make sure your business is tagged correctly.

  5. Track the results

    You receive a short report on views, reach and the enquiries the collaboration brought in.

What it costs

₹2,000

Bond Craft Media agency fee

Covers finding the creators, briefing them, coordinating the shoot and posting, and reporting.

plus

The influencer's own fee

Every creator sets their own rate based on their reach and the content. It goes to the influencer and is confirmed with you before booking.
